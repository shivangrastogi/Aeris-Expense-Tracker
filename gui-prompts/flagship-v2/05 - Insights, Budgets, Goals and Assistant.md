Using the **AERIS design system already created in this project** (Graphite dark + Porcelain light, steel-blue accent, Plus Jakarta Sans, rising-line-and-dot motif, muted colour-blind-safe category palette, no neon, no emoji), design these screens on a Pixel 8 frame (412 × 915 dp), in **both Dark and Light**, as a clickable prototype (Insights tab active in the floating bottom navigation where relevant).

Sample month (₹): spent ₹39,153; saved ₹45,800; avg per transaction ₹1,300; categories Rent ₹4,000 (your share), Groceries ₹6,022, Food ₹5,116, Shopping ₹4,698, Travel ₹2,979, Bills ₹1,849, Entertainment ₹649, Health ₹560. Budgets: total ₹60,000; Food cap ₹9,000; Travel ₹5,000; Shopping ₹4,000 (over by ₹698). Goals: Goa trip ₹18,000 of ₹40,000 by 20 Dec; Emergency fund ₹62,000 of ₹1,50,000.

## 1. Insights tab
Large title "Insights", segmented control **Charts · Tips · Future**, range pill "This month ▾" (presets: This month, Last month, Last 3 months, This year, Custom).
- **Charts**: three stat tiles (Spent, Saved green, Avg/txn); donut "Spending by category" with centre total and a legend with direct labels (tap a slice → filtered list); "Spending trend" cumulative area line vs last month (dashed grey); "Daily spending" heat-map calendar; "Top merchants" ranked bars; "Income vs spending" monthly bars for 6 months.
- **Tips**: "Aeris says" card with one data-driven insight at a time ("At this pace Food goes over budget in 6 days", "Unusual: ₹2,199 at Myntra — 3× your usual") with "1 of 4 · tap for next" and "Ask Aeris a question"; predicted month-end spend card (₹46.2K, range ₹41–51K); budget projections list; recurring payments found; savings recommendations with potential saving per month.
- **Future**: "Future you" — projection chart of net worth to Dec 2027 (₹8.1L), adjustable monthly saving slider that updates the line, goal milestones on the line.

## 2. Budgets
Hero card "Total monthly budget" with the rising-line progress (₹39,873 of ₹60,000 · ₹20,127 left), "Edit". Card "Roll unused budget into next month" (switch) + "Or move ₹16,510 into a goal". "Per-category caps" list: category tile, name, "₹5,116 of ₹9,000", thin progress bar in the category hue (red when over), chevron; categories without caps show "No cap set — currently ₹6,022" and "Set cap". AI suggestion chip "Suggest budgets from my history".

## 3. Budget edit
Category header, big amount input, slider, quick picks (Last month's spend ₹4,700, 3-month average ₹5,050), "Alert me at 80%" switch, Save with discard guard.

## 4. Goals
List of goal cards (icon tile, title, progress ring, ₹18,000 of ₹40,000, "₹2,750/week to make it by 20 Dec"), "Add money" and ⋮. New goal sheet: name, target, deadline, icon picker, auto-save rule ("Move round-ups weekly"). Completed goal celebration state (calm, a tick and the rising line reaching the dot — no confetti explosion).

## 5. Net worth
Hero: ₹6,42,310 with breakdown chips (Cash in accounts ₹97,956 · Goals ₹80,000 · Owed to you ₹5,500 · You owe ₹0); 12-month line chart; account list; "Projection" line with the saving pace.

## 6. Report card (monthly)
Big grade "A−" in a calm circle; three score bars (Budget 34/40, Savings 36/40, Habits 15/20) with one-line reasons; "What went well" / "Try next month"; "Share" (creates an image card without amounts if privacy mode is on).

## 7. Monthly Wrapped (story)
8 full-screen story slides with a progress segments bar and tap-to-advance: intro "October in money", total spent, top category, top merchant, biggest day, savings rate, a streak/goal highlight, share card. Each slide uses one muted category hue as its background tone (not saturated), big type, minimal charts.

## 8. Ask Aeris (assistant chat)
Header "Ask Aeris" with the small "A" logo; privacy line "Answered on your phone, privately". Suggested prompt chips ("How much did I spend this month?", "What's my biggest expense?", "Can I afford a ₹40K phone in December?"). Conversation with rich answer cards: a mini bar chart, a category breakdown list, an affordability verdict card with a reason. Input bar with mic and send; voice input state.
