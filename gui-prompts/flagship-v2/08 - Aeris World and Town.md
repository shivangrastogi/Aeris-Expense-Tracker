Using the **AERIS design system already created in this project** (Graphite dark + Porcelain light, steel-blue accent, Plus Jakarta Sans, muted palette, no neon, no emoji in UI chrome), design the light game layer on a Pixel 8 frame (412 × 915 dp), in **both Dark and Light**. It rewards good money habits; it must feel premium and calm — closer to a beautifully crafted city-builder miniature (think Monument Valley's restraint) than a loud mobile game. Currency: "Aura" (earned from check-ins, staying under budget, finishing goals) and rare "Gems" (milestones only).

## 1. Aeris World (hub)
Header with back, customise action and Aura balance pill (⚡ 420). Equipped character card: a refined minimal character (geometric, softly lit, not cartoonish — like a small sculpted figure) named "Saver Sprout", "Level 3 · Hatchling tier", XP bar in the rising-line style. "Aeris Town" preview card (isometric miniature at dusk) with "Enter →". Segmented Avatars · Quests.
- Avatars grid: cards with rarity label (Common grey, Rare steel, Epic iris, Legendary ochre, Mythic rose — all muted), name, tagline, state Equipped / Equip / locked with Aura cost.
- Quests: weekly challenges ("No-spend weekend", "Stay under Food budget 7 days", "Log every expense today") with progress and rewards; boss challenges ("Defeat the Impulse Buyer: no shopping for 10 days") with a progress health bar.

## 2. Aeris Town (full screen)
An isometric 7×7 tile town at dusk/night on a deep graphite sky with faint stars and a soft moon. Buildings as elegant low-poly miniatures with warm lit windows: Town hall, Houses, Farm, Market, Vault, Tower, Fountain, Statue, Trees. HUD: back, "Aeris Town Lv 3", help; stat chips (Aura, Gems, Builders 1/2); weather banner ("Checked in today · collections ×1.5" calm green, or "Over budget this month · storm ×0.5" muted red with rain). Bottom bar: "Collect 120 Aura" button and "Build".
- Build sheet: building cards (cost, time, what it produces), affordability states.
- Placement mode: valid tiles outlined in steel blue, banner "Tap a tile to place the Market · Cancel".
- Building panel: level, production per day, "Upgrade (2 h · 300 Aura)", "Rush (1 gem)", "Remove".
- Under-construction building with a timer chip.
- Pinch-zoom and pan affordances (zoomed-in variant).

## 3. Challenges, Future self, Bosses
Consistent card-based screens: challenge detail with daily progress dots and reward; "Future self" letter screen (a message to your future self with a projection chart); boss detail with the boss's minimal emblem, conditions and a calm progress bar.

Keep all game colour muted and harmonious with the app; the game must never look like a different app.
