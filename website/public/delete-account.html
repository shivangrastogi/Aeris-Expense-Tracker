Delete your AERIS Expense account and data

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open AERIS Expense and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account & security → Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and confirm.

This permanently deletes your transactions, budgets, goals, loans, subscriptions, shared bills, receipts, profile, encryption keys and your sign-in account. It cannot be undone.

Can't open the app?

Email shivangrastogi73@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete all data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is kept

Nothing linked to you is kept after deletion. Anonymous crash reports without personal or transaction data may be retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
