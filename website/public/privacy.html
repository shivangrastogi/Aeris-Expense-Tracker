AERIS Expense – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

AERIS Expense ("AERIS", "the app") is an expense tracker. It is built so that only you can read your financial data. Your data is processed on your phone and end-to-end encrypted before it is synced. We do not sell, rent or share your data, and the app shows no ads.

Data the app accesses

  • SMS messages (optional, with your permission). The app reads SMS on your device to find bank, card and UPI transaction alerts (amount, merchant, date) and add them as transactions. Only messages that look like transaction alerts are used; other messages are ignored and not stored. SMS text is processed on your device and is not uploaded.
  • Notifications from payment apps (optional). If you enable notification access, the app reads payment notifications from UPI apps (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) to record payments. Other notifications are ignored. This is processed on your device.
  • Camera / photos (optional). To scan a receipt or set a profile photo. Images are compressed and encrypted before storage.
  • Microphone (optional). For voice entry of a transaction, using Android's speech recognition.
  • Account information. Your email address and display name, used to sign you in.
  • Financial data you create or that is detected: transactions, budgets, goals, loans, subscriptions, shared bills, receipts and settings.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, a technical report without your transaction data may be sent (Firebase Crashlytics).

How data is stored

Your financial data is encrypted on your phone with a key that only you hold, then stored in your account on Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics) so it can sync across your devices. Firebase stores only encrypted data that we cannot read, plus your email and basic account details. Your encryption key is protected by your password and a recovery key that only you see; we cannot reset it or read your data.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal or financial data with third parties. Google Firebase acts as our service provider to store encrypted data and sign you in.

Your choices

  • You can withdraw any permission at any time in Android settings.
  • You can export your data to Excel or an encrypted backup from Settings.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all data in the app (Settings → Account & security → Delete account), or request deletion at /delete-account.html.

Security and retention

Data is encrypted in transit and end-to-end encrypted at rest. We keep your data until you delete your account; deletion removes your data from our servers.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 18.
